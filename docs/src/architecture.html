Tablecast · how a plan is made

Read the week, write the plan, check every cited claim

Every step runs on the model that suits it, on Nebius Token Factory. Times and costs: the Gjelina replay (Quick, recorded 21 Oct 2026).

Input

The owner

  • Restaurant and city
  • This week’s goal
  • Optional: a photo of the menu
  • Writer: Quick or Economy

1 · Context, in parallel · ~1 s

The week and the block, from open data

  • 7-day forecast at the door · public holidays
  • Events within 5 km · every venue within 600 m
  • Menu photo read into dishes and prices

OpenStreetMapOpen-MeteoNager.DateTicketmasterNemotron 3.5 Super VL

2 · Research · ~30 s · $0.001 + 4 Tavily credits

The live web: menu, guests, local news, a rival

  • Map lists the site’s menu pages; code keeps this branch’s; Extract reads it
  • A fast agent loop searches and reads (≤ 6 searches, 4 reads, in parallel)
  • Results about a same-name place elsewhere are set aside

Nemotron 3.5 Lightning · reasoning offTavily Map · Search · Extract

Source ledger Everything read becomes a numbered source, cut into numbered sentences (S9.4): the forecast, events, holidays, the block, menu and review pages, news. Claims can only cite these.

3 · Write · Quick ~30 s · $0.047

The plan, as one forced function call

  • Strict schema, streamed: the UI drafts it live
  • Claims cite sentence ids, never a typed quote
  • Loop guard, time budget, one retry

Nemotron 3 Ultra · QuickNemotron 3 Super · Economy

Economy (Super): ~2 min, a third to half the cost

4 · Verify · code decides

Every cited claim, every price, every date

  • Cited sentence exists; every number is in it
  • Its words are there (another language: numbers and names)
  • About this restaurant; an add-on goes with its own dish
  • Prices in the text match a dish or a checked claim
  • No date before today, local time

deterministic · no model judges truth

Repair, once · ~12 s · $0.031

Fix or drop what failed

  • One small call per failed claim, in parallel
  • One call for unbacked prices, one for past dates
  • Re-checked by code; what still fails is shown as unverified

Nemotron 3 Ultra · low effort

Output

The owner’s week

  • Demand moments, 3 dishes to test, an event
  • Post, supplier order, day-by-day plan
  • Evidence chips and the engine room: every call, its tokens, time and cost
